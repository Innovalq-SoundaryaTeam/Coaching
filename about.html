Our Mission

Making rank-worthy coaching accessible to every serious aspirant

LS Academy was founded on a simple belief: a student's pin code shouldn't determine the quality of coaching they receive. We combine experienced, exam-cleared faculty with a technology platform that tracks every student's progress in granular detail — so no one falls through the cracks.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted name in competitive exam mentorship by 2030.

Our Values

Transparency in results, rigor in teaching, and empathy for every learner's pace.

Our Journey

From one classroom to a nationwide institute

2012
A Single Classroom in Delhi

LS Academy began as a 20-student banking-exam batch run by Dr. Arvind Menon out of a rented classroom.

2016
Expansion to SSC & Civil Services

Added dedicated SSC and UPSC programmes as demand grew beyond banking aspirants.

2019
First Online Batches Launched

Introduced live online classes, reaching students beyond our 4 physical centers for the first time.

2022
Adaptive Test Engine Introduced

Built our in-house mock-test platform with topic-wise analytics, now used by 24,000+ students.

2025
18 Centers, 5 Exam Categories

Today LS Academy operates 18 centers nationwide across Banking, SSC, UPSC, JEE and NEET coaching.
